Write three functions for doing TreeDeltas of a BinaryTree of Ints
 A TreeDelta of a BinaryTree of ints is defined as
	The Delta between the TreeDeltas of the subtrees
	    and the current node's value

PreOrderTreeDelta:
	CurNode - PreOrderTreeDelta(ltree) - PreOrderTreeDelta(rTree)
InOrderTreeDelta:
	InOrderTreeDelta(lTree) - CurNode - InOrderTreeDelta(rTree)
PostOrderTreeDelta:
	PostOrderTreeDelta(lTree) - PostOrderTreeDelta(rTree) - CurNode
